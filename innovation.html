Innovation

Making room for practical improvement

Innovation is useful when it addresses a real project need. Evaluate a new approach against the site requirements and the support needed over its working life.

Begin with a problem

Identify what needs to improve, such as operating visibility, use of space or maintenance access.

Evaluate the proposal

Ask for evidence of suitability, clear assumptions and an explanation of any uncertainties.

Learn from operation
