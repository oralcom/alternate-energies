Regional Focus

Understand the location behind your solar project.

Africa ↗

Africa includes many different energy markets and project environments. Our stated service coverage is East Africa; enquiries concerning other African locations can be discussed without assuming local delivery availability.

England ↗

We welcome enquiries for solar installation, maintenance, rooftop partnerships and direct solar supply in London and Norwich. Our registered office is in Norwich.

East Africa ↗

We welcome solar enquiries from all East African countries. Tell us your exact city and country so that access, site requirements and project arrangements can be discussed.
