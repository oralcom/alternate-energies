A few useful answers.

Is this a grid electricity tariff?

This service concerns power supplied directly from our solar systems. It is not a general grid electricity switching service.

What happens when solar generation is low?

Solar output varies with daylight and weather. Your proposal needs to address how remaining electricity demand is met; uninterrupted supply from solar alone is not promised.
