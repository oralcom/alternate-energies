Energy Transition

People, technology and planning behind the move to solar energy.

Outlook ↗

A useful energy outlook starts with the needs of a property or organisation. It connects today's electricity use with future plans, rather than treating solar as an isolated purchase.

Partnerships ↗

Solar projects bring together property owners, customers and delivery teams. Clear responsibilities help each participant understand how the project will be assessed and progressed.

Policy ↗

Every project needs its own review of the permissions and requirements that apply at its location. This page outlines the questions to raise; it does not provide country-specific regulatory guidance.

Technology ↗

Technology choices should follow the project requirements. A discussion about equipment starts with the available space, expected electricity use and how the installation will be supported.

Socio-economic Impact ↗

The value of a project extends to the people who use the property and take part in its delivery. Any claims about, savings or community benefits should be supported by project evidence.

Country Engagement ↗

A country-level conversation begins with the specific location, stakeholders and needs behind a project. Our enquiry process welcomes this context before considering the next step.

Project Facilitation ↗

A project is easier to discuss when the information, responsibilities and next decisions are clear. Use an initial conversation to identify the work needed before a proposal can be considered.

Planning ↗

Good preparation helps connect a solar idea with the practical requirements of the site. Timing should reflect the assessments and decisions needed for the individual project.

Innovation ↗

Innovation is useful when it addresses a real project need. Evaluate a new approach against the site requirements and the support needed over its working life.

Finance & Investment ↗

Solar proposals need a clear commercial scope. This section is a discussion guide, not an investment offer, financing commitment or financial recommendation.
