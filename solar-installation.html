Solar installation across East Africa,
London and Norwich.

01

Start with your property

Tell us whether your project is a home, business or new development. Roof space, shading and the way you use electricity help shape the conversation.

02

Plan the right system

A site assessment helps establish the suitable layout, system size and installation requirements. Your proposal should explain the scope, costs and next steps.

03

Installation and handover

Agree the installation plan and site access before work begins. At handover, discuss system operation, documentation and ongoing maintenance.

Make your first
conversation count.

You do not need every detail to get started. Share what you have, and we can take it from there.

Helpful information to share

  • Your city and country
  • Property type and roof photographs, if available
  • Recent electricity usage or bills
  • Your preferred project timeline

A few useful answers.

Can I get a price before a site assessment?

We can discuss your requirements first. A project-specific quotation depends on the property, system scope and any site assessment needed.

Will solar cover all my electricity use?

That depends on system size, sunlight and when you use electricity. We can discuss the expected contribution as part of the proposal; full independence is not assumed.
