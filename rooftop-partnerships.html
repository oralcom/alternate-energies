Home / Our solutions / Commercial rooftop partnerships

More potential above your business.

An unused commercial rooftop can be the beginning of a solar partnership. Explore what could work for your building and organisation.

A clear path forward.

Commercial rooftop partnerships across East Africa,
London and Norwich.

01

Explore the opportunity

Commercial property owners and authorised occupiers can start a conversation about available roof space and energy needs.

02

Assess the practicalities

Roof condition, structure, access, ownership and electricity arrangements all matter. An assessment helps establish whether a partnership is feasible.

03

Agree a clear arrangement

Discuss responsibilities, access, maintenance, duration and commercial terms. The right arrangement depends on the site and is agreed individually.

Make your first
conversation count.

You do not need every detail to get started. Share what you have, and we can take it from there.

Helpful information to share

  • Property location and building use
  • Approximate roof area and condition
  • Ownership or lease arrangements
  • Your business electricity requirements

A few useful answers.

Is every commercial roof suitable?

Suitability depends on the roof structure, condition, shading, access and other site requirements. We assess the opportunity before proposing a partnership.

What are the commercial terms?

Terms are specific to each project. Contact us to discuss the site; no standard rent, savings or financing arrangement is assumed.
